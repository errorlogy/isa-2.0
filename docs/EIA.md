## Definition

**EIA** (Endogenous Initiative Architecture) is the research layer for **prompt-free, intervention-measured motive formation** that must survive a twelve-context evidence panel, heterogeneous sheaf consistency, multiscale collapse guards, and executable NAMM certificates before any **reversible research action** is allowed.

EIA answers: *under what mechanical conditions may a system propose and execute a bounded research step without external prompt coercion?*

ISA answers a complementary question: *how do institutional constraints and symbolic carriers align when multi-agent consensus is modeled?*

| Layer | Focus | Typical output label |
|-------|-------|----------------------|
| **EIA** | Initiative, evidence, fail-closed gates | `COMPUTATIONAL_EVIDENCE` |
| **ISA** | Institutional–symbolic bind, 12+1 topology | `INSTITUTIONAL_MODEL` |

Key design choices:

- **12 nodes** = complete \(4 \times 3\) factor grid — not a sacred constant
- **Graph** = \(K_4 \square K_3\) (shared domain or role adjacency)
- **Hermeneutic firewall** — symbolic material isolated from truth/action gates
- **Fail-closed** — common-mode consensus and missing nodes → ABSTAIN
