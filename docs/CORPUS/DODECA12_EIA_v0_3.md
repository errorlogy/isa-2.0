## What DODECA-12 is

DODECA-12 v0.3 is a **fail-closed, real-agent-capable research harness** that runs twelve isolated reasoning contexts through evidence sealing, heterogeneous sheaf consistency, persistent topology, multiscale coherence guards, endogenous motive formation, and executable NAMM certificates before any bounded research action is allowed.

### 4 × 3 grid = 12 (not a sacred constant)

Twelve nodes occupy the complete product of four epistemic domains and three procedural roles:

| Domains \(D\) | Roles \(R\) |
|---------------|-------------|
| empirical | generator |
| causal | critic |
| formal / NAMM | verifier |
| normative | |

\[
N_0 = |D \times R| = 4 \times 3 = 12
\]

This is an **executable minimum for complete factor coverage** of the declared grid — not proof that 12 is universally optimal for all architectures.

### Communication topology: \(K_4 \square K_3\)

Nodes are labeled by \((d, r) \in D \times R\). Two nodes are adjacent when they share a domain **or** a role:

\[
A_{uv} = \mathbf{1}[d_u = d_v \lor r_u = r_v], \qquad G = K_4 \square K_3
\]

The v0.3 implementation certifies: 12 nodes, 30 edges, regular degree 5, 16 triangles, algebraic connectivity \(\lambda_2 = 3\), Laplacian spectrum \(\{0^1, 3^2, 4^3, 7^6\}\) — see NAMM-DODECA-GRAPH-001 in the raw bundle.
