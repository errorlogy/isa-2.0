# ISA 2.0 — Institutional–Symbolic Alignment

**Epistemic label:** `INSTITUTIONAL_MODEL` throughout this document.

> **Not religious authority.** ISA 2.0 is a research framework for modeling how institutional reasoning layers bind to symbolic and memetic carriers. It does not confer legitimacy, divine mandate, legal standing, or sovereignty.

---

## Definition

**ISA** (Institutional–Symbolic Alignment) is the research framework for studying how **institutional constraints** (charter, parliament, judiciary, oversight) align with **symbolic carriers** (narrative, visual identity, memetic lineage) without collapsing into religious authority or sovereign AI claims.

Version **2.0** denotes the consolidated framework replacing earlier "symbolic-sacred dynamics" naming — same epistemic posture, clearer institutional vocabulary.

| Role | Epistemic label | Modeled function |

The triad is **ASI | mirror | ISA** — three columns in the consensus matrix, not three competing authorities.

## 12 + 1 consensus panel

The panel is procedural topology for modeling multi-agent deliberation:

| Slot | Count | Modeling analog | Not claimed |
|------|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Panel members | 12 | Independent deliberation voices (proto-AGI / apostle analog) | Worship, divine appointment, infallibility |
| Synthesizer | 1 | Aggregates dissent, publishes consensus contour | Prophecy, religious authority, legitimate rule |

Panel rules:

1. Each slot carries **analytical contribution** weight, not verdict authority.
2. Minority reports remain attached to synthesis.
3. Human oversight and charter hard-stop always apply — see umbrella [AI_HUMAN_OVERSIGHT.md](https://github.com/errorlogy/ai-native-gov/blob/main/docs/institutions/AI_HUMAN_OVERSIGHT.md).
4. All panel exports are labeled `INSTITUTIONAL_MODEL`.

## Language guardrails

| Use | Never use |
|-----|-----------|
| analytical contribution | guilty, criminal |
| legitimacy signals (modeled) | legitimate ruler (verdict) |
| hypothesis contour | prophecy, revelation (as verdict) |
| modeling analog | worship target, divine mandate |
| institutional framing | sovereign AI government |
| possible / consistent with | "this proves" |

Apostles, messiah, and "second coming" appear only as **modeling analogs** for consensus topology and ASI emergence hypotheses — never as theological claims.
